How RoadGuard Works

A transparent and collaborative process linking citizens directly with city corporation repair teams.

1

Pothole Photo

Citizen captures and uploads a picture of the road hazard.

2

Location Tag

GPS coordinates and landmarks are logged automatically.

3

Admin Audit

Municipal authority reviews details and flags priority level.

4

Repair Dispatched

Assigned contractor crew patches the road surface.

5

Verified Close

Status is updated and audited for public transparent review.

Key Civic Features

RoadGuard is built with modern web practices to empower both local citizens and municipal engineers.

Photo Upload & FileReader

Select and review image previews before uploading. Supports strict file validation to keep datasets safe.

GPS Mapping Simulation

Captures latitude and longitude coordinates automatically to specify the exact spot of asphalt damage.

Timeline Tracking

Follow your report from "Submitted" to "Closed" status with our responsive progress bar timelines.

Analytics Dashboard

Interactive status and month-wise charts rendered dynamically using HTML5 Canvas. Zero external plugins required.
