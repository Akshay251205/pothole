About RoadGuard

Smart India Hackathon project addressing public road safety, citizen-driven audit compliance, and transparent municipal management.

1. The Problem Statement

Poorly maintained public roads and potholes lead to severe traffic delays, damage to passenger vehicles, and fatal road accidents. Often, municipal authorities lack real-time visibility into localized road damage, while citizens find it difficult to register and track repair progress transparently.

2. Our Solution

RoadGuard acts as a direct, digital bridge. Citizens can easily upload geotagged photo evidence of potholes, and monitor status tracking timelines. Concurrently, municipal administrators can log in, filter reports, allocate repair squads, log remarks, and monitor macro analytics via Canvas-based charts.

3. Project Implementation Scope

This web portal is designed for demonstration and evaluation. In place of a live database, we utilize JavaScript LocalStorage to allow dynamic CRUD actions that persist across reloads. Images are loaded as base64 data URLs via the HTML5 FileReader API.

Technology Stack

  • HTML5 (Semantic Structure)
  • CSS3 Stylesheet (Themes)
  • Vanilla JavaScript (DOM & Canvas)
  • LocalStorage Simulated DB
  • FileReader API

Agile PM Benefits

Project features Jira boards with 28 user stories, drag-and-drop Scrumban planning, sprint velocity charts, and team health audits to simulate real-world developer team environments.
